Built around your programme

Your programme. Your requirements. The right artists.

Whether you are planning a concert, festival, dance production, rangapravesham, temple celebration or corporate event, we can curate an ensemble around the specific needs of your programme.

From a single accompanying musician to a complete orchestra, and even dancers and recording support, the team can be assembled and coordinated through our network of experienced artists.

Artists & ensembles

The artists your programme needs.

Choose one specialist or bring several disciplines together as a complete performance team.

Vocalists

Lead and supporting Carnatic vocalists, additional singers and vocal ensembles for concerts, dance productions and special performances.

Instrumentalists & accompanists

Violin, flute, veena, keyboard, harmonium and other instrumental accompaniment, selected to complement the repertoire and format of the programme.

Rhythm & dance accompaniment

Mridangam, tabla, ghatam, kanjira, morsing, nattuvangam and other percussion and rhythmic accompaniment for concerts and classical dance productions.

Dancers & complete productions

Classical dancers and performance artists can also be brought together for festivals, cultural showcases, corporate events and specially curated productions.
