Chapter 01

Music was the first language.

Born into a family of musicians and Kathakali artists in ShreeKrishnapuram, Kerala, he grew up inside the tradition. His first solo Carnatic performance came in childhood at the Eashwara Mangalam Mahaganapathy Temple.

Chapter 02

Inheritance became discipline.

He earned a Diploma in Carnatic Vocal from The Lalitha Kalalayam with distinction. He trained under Shri K. M. Krishnan in the Guru-Shishya Parampara and received expert Sopanam training from his father, Shri V. N. Narayanan Namboodiri.

Chapter 03

The voice became a responsibility.

He has performed on All India Radio and Doordarshan, and has been an integral lead vocalist for dance schools, productions and arangetrams across India. International work is presented through artistic collaborations, alongside Carnatic concerts, fusion music and live jugalbandis.

Teacher, mentor, founder

Preserving the tradition, and passing it on.

He is the Founder and Director of Shrutilaya Fine Arts, Vashi, through which he conducts festivals of dance and music, supports India's diverse cultural heritage and gives a platform to upcoming artistes.

He has served as a faculty member at the Bharata College of Fine Arts & Research Centre, Dadar, and continues to teach beginners and professional singers, judge music competitions and serve as an examiner for Carnatic vocal examinations. He also conducts customised Music Therapy workshops.
